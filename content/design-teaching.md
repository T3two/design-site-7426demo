# Design Teaching

Engineering is more than making a part fit. This is where we explain the thinking behind our choices, the tradeoffs we encounter, and the tools we use to turn sketches into mechanisms.

## Start with the process

- [Design Philosophy](?page=design-philosophy) — how we define a problem, prototype ideas, and improve a design through iteration.
- [CAD & FeatureScript](?page=cad-featurescript) — working parametrically and building repeatable CAD workflows.
- [CAD Library](?page=cad-library) — explore the public models and assemblies in 3D.

## Learn the hardware

- [Power Transmission](?page=power-transmission) — moving power through a mechanism.
- [FRC Motors](?page=motors) — comparing electromechanical options.
- [Gears & Shafts](?page=gears-and-shafts) — the interfaces that hold moving systems together.
